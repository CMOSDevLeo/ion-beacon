Ion Beacon

Project Overview

Physikalisches Feedback-Instrument

Ion Beacon translates the invisible forces of snowboarding — weightlessness, G-forces, impact — into visible light to visually amplify the dynamics of a ride, especially for co-riders but also for the rider themselves.

Built for personal use and friends who appreciate tech-enhanced riding with cool, latency-free reactive lighting.
